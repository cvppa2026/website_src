### Keynote 2: Fumio Okura
*Associate Professor, Osaka University*

**Plant Phenotyping as a Frontier for Computer Vision**

**Abstract:**  
Plant phenotyping (PP) poses a range of challenging computer vision (CV) problems, which cannot be fully addressed by simply applying existing CV techniques. In this talk, I will present several case studies from my own research experience, illustrating how problems arising from PP can be formulated as CV problems and developed into contributions to the CV community.
